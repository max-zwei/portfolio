FOUR DECADES — QUICK START

Copy the entire agents/four-decade-poster-agent.txt file into your writing agent's instructions, or paste it at the beginning of a new conversation. All four reviewed voice definitions are embedded; no extra files are required. This is a reusable agent instruction file, not a separately deployed service.

EXAMPLE REQUESTS
Write a title introducing a poster about how we spend our time. Maximum 5 words per version.

Write a paragraph introducing sleep, paid work, unpaid work and leisure as parts of a 24-hour day. Around 45 words per version. No numerical findings yet.

Write a paragraph explaining these fictional test figures: surveyed 15–29-year-olds averaged 2 hours of daily leisure in 2004 and 3 hours in 2024. Do not invent a cause. Maximum 50 words per version.

Only rewrite the 1990s version. Make it less cynical and keep the facts.

DEFAULT BEHAVIOR
Four labeled versions, in decade order. A title request produces titles only. A paragraph request produces paragraphs only. Asking for both produces both. All versions use the same supplied facts and central message. Explicit limits and decade selections take precedence.

PROVENANCE
Voice modules and shared data safeguards come from the four poster-edition v3.5 prompts. New instructions handle format selection, shared content and comparison across the four outputs.
